# Algeria Compass — Brand Guide

**Domain:** https://algeriacompass.com
**Category:** Independent travel-intelligence platform dedicated entirely to Algeria.
**Reference points:** Lonely Planet, Atlas Obscura, National Geographic Travel — but focused exclusively on Algeria.

---

## Identity statement
Algeria Compass is the definitive, independent guide to discovering Algeria — a travel-intelligence platform that maps the whole country as a connected body of knowledge, not a catalogue of trips.

## Positioning statement
For travellers, researchers and the curious who want to understand Algeria in depth, Algeria Compass is the independent reference platform that turns the largest, least-documented country in Africa into clear, trustworthy, openly sourced knowledge — because reliable English-language information about Algeria is scarce, and the country deserves better.

## Brand promise
Everything we publish is researched, independently reviewed, openly sourced, and kept up to date — and we correct it in the open when we are wrong.

---

## Brand mission
To make Algeria genuinely understandable to the world, and to become the most trusted guide to the country in any language.

## Brand vision
A complete, sourced, living map of Algeria — all 58 provinces and their major sites, landscapes and cultures — that is the first place anyone turns to understand or plan a journey to the country.

## Brand values
1. **Independent** — no sponsored placements; recommendations reflect traveller value, never our margins.
2. **Trustworthy** — published standards, independent review, named sources, open corrections.
3. **Respectful** — Algeria and its peoples are treated with seriousness and cultural care, never exoticised.
4. **Curious & rigorous** — we go deep, check facts, and show our work.
5. **Useful** — knowledge is paired with practical, accurate guidance.

---

## The four missions

**Editorial mission** — Publish accurate, in-depth, well-written guides to a published standard; review them independently; cite sources; update and correct openly.

**Knowledge mission** — Model Algeria as a structured knowledge graph — provinces, heritage, landscapes, food and culture as connected entities — so relationships are explicit and machine-readable, and the platform stays consistent as it grows.

**Cultural mission** — Represent Algeria's Amazigh, Arab, Saharan, Ottoman, Andalusian and Mediterranean heritage with depth and respect, foregrounding local voices and history.

**Travel mission** — Help people travel Algeria with understanding: when a reader is ready, we can also help shape a route or arrange a guided journey with trusted local operators — but the guide comes first and stands on its own.
