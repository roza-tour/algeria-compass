Algiers Day Tour — Hamma Garden, Notre-Dame d'Afrique & Army Museum

Overview

A relaxed alternative to the Casbah day, this private tour takes in three Algiers landmarks at an easy pace: the lush Jardin d'Essai du Hamma, one of the great botanical gardens of the Mediterranean; the hilltop basilica of Notre-Dame d'Afrique with its sweeping view over the bay; and the Army Museum's account of Algeria's history. A traditional lunch rounds it off, with hotel pickup throughout.

Highlights

  • The historic Hamma botanical garden
  • Sea views from Notre-Dame d'Afrique
  • Algeria's military history at the Army Museum
  • A traditional Algerian lunch
  • Private guiding with hotel pickup

Why this trip is different

  • The green side of AlgiersThe Hamma garden is a tranquil, beautiful counterpoint to the busy city.
  • A view worth the climbNotre-Dame d'Afrique looks out over the whole bay.
  • Easy and privateA gentle pace with your own guide and vehicle — ideal for a first or relaxed day.
  • Lunch includedA traditional Algerian meal between the sights.
