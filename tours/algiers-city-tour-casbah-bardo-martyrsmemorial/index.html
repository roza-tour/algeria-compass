Overview

Algiers wears its history on the hillside. In a single day you climb through the UNESCO-listed Casbah — the dense Ottoman medina of stepped lanes, courtyard houses and old mosques above the bay — take in the archaeology and art of the Bardo National Museum, and stand beneath the soaring Martyrs' Memorial for the city's best view over the white houses and the Mediterranean. A traditional lunch breaks the day. It is the ideal first day in Algeria: the capital's layers, read end to end with a local guide.

Tour FAQ

Is the Casbah safe to visit?

Yes — with a local guide it is a rewarding, welcoming walk; the lanes are steep, so wear good shoes.

Is lunch included?

Yes — a traditional Algerian lunch at a local restaurant is part of the day.

Can you collect me from the airport?

Yes — pickup and drop-off at your Algiers hotel or the airport is included.

How much walking is there?

A fair amount, much of it on the Casbah's steps and slopes, at an easy pace with stops.
