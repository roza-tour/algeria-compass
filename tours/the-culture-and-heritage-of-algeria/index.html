Culture & Heritage of Algeria — 8 Days

Eight days across Algeria's culture and history — the capital and its Casbah, Roman Tipaza and Djémila, the bridges of Constantine, and the oasis town of Bou Saada.

Highlights

  • Algiers, its museums and the historic Casbah
  • Roman Tipaza, Cherchell and Djémila
  • Constantine and its suspension bridge
  • The oasis charm of Bou Saada
  • A blend of culture, history and landscape

Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 1 Arrival in Algiers

    Airport welcome, check-in at a city-center hotel, an evening city tour and dinner.

  2. Day 2 Algiers cultural tour

    The Bardo Museum, the UNESCO Casbah, and the Martyrs' Memorial (Makam Echahid).

  3. Day 3 Tipaza & Cherchell

    The Royal Mausoleum of Mauretania, the Tipaza archaeological park, and the Cherchell museum.

  4. Day 4 Constantine

    The Sidi M'Cid bridge, Ahmed Bey Palace, the Emir Abdelkader Mosque, and a city walk.

  5. Day 5 Djémila

    The UNESCO Roman site of Djémila — its theatre and temples — with a local lunch.

  6. Day 6 To Bou Saada

    The road from Constantine to Bou Saada, check-in and free time, and a traditional dinner with local music.

  7. Day 7 Bou Saada & return

    The Bou Saada waterfall, the Ferrero Mill, and the El Hamel zawiya, then the return to Algiers.

  8. Day 8 Departure

    Breakfast, check-out and transfer to Algiers airport.
