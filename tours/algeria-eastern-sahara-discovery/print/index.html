Private tour itinerary

Eastern Algeria & Sahara Discovery — 6 Days

6 days · 5 nights · From €912 per person

Six days across Algeria's east — Constantine's bridges, the Roman cities of Djémila and Timgad, a Saharan gateway at Bou Saada, and the Béjaïa coast. Private trip on your own dates, with a licensed local guide.

Day by day

Day 1Algiers to Constantine

Arrive in Algiers and transfer to Constantine, with its suspension bridges, the Emir Abdelkader Mosque and the old town; overnight in Constantine.

  • Constantine's bridges — The 'City of Bridges' spanning the dizzying Rhumel gorge, the Sidi M'Cid footbridge slung high above it.
  • The Emir Abdelkader Mosque — One of Africa’s largest mosques, its twin minarets a modern landmark of Constantine.
  • The Souika old town — The tight lanes of Constantine’s old medina clinging to the rock.

Day 2Constantine to Sétif

Travel to Sétif and the UNESCO Roman ruins of Djémila, then explore the city center; overnight in Sétif.

  • Djémila (Cuicul) — A UNESCO Roman city in green mountains, superbly preserved — forum, temples and a steep theatre.
  • The Djémila mosaics — On-site mosaics among the richest in the Roman world, in the site museum.
  • Aïn El Fouara, Sétif — The beloved 1898 fountain at the city's heart, Sétif's enduring symbol.

Day 3Sétif to Batna

Continue to Batna and the UNESCO Roman city of Timgad, with time at local heritage sites; overnight in Batna.

  • Timgad — Trajan's grid-planned colony of AD 100 — the 'Pompeii of the Aurès', laid out across the open plain.
  • Trajan's Arch — The triumphal arch framing Timgad's main street — the city's iconic silhouette.
  • The Timgad library & theatre — The ruins of a rare Roman public library and a 3,500-seat theatre still in use today.

Day 4Batna to Biskra to Bou Saada

Cross to Biskra, the gateway to the Sahara, for its palm groves and oasis scenery, then on to Bou Saada and its traditional markets; overnight in Bou Saada.

  • Biskra palm groves — The 'Queen of the Ziban' — a great date-palm oasis marking the true start of the Sahara.
  • Bou Saada — The 'City of Happiness', an oasis town long beloved of painters.
  • Bou Saada souks — Traditional markets for silver, wool and dates.

Day 5Bou Saada to Algiers

Visit Zaouia El Hamel and nearby heritage, then transfer to Algiers for a free evening; overnight in Algiers.

  • The El Hamel zawiya — A hilltop Rahmaniyya Sufi shrine and Quranic school above Bou Saada — calm, austere and panoramic.
  • Bou Saada and the Ouled Naïl foothills — The "city of happiness" — a palm oasis at the foot of bare ochre hills, and a painters’ town since the 19th century.
  • The road north to Algiers — Up out of the steppe across the Hodna plain and over the Tell Atlas, arriving in the capital for a free evening.

Day 6Béjaïa & departure

A day trip to Béjaïa for its fortress, Cap Carbon and coastal views, then the transfer to the airport.

  • Gouraya fort, Béjaïa — A fort high above Béjaïa with sweeping Kabyle-coast views.
  • Cap Carbon — A dramatic cape and lighthouse on one of the Mediterranean's most beautiful coastlines.
  • The Béjaïa corniche — The cliff road between Gouraya and Cap Carbon, above the coves that made this stretch of the Kabyle coast famous.
