Bou Saada — 2-Day Oasis Escape

Two days at Bou Saada, the 'City of Happiness' — a desert waterfall, craft markets, a Bedouin dinner with music, and a famous Sufi shrine.

Overview

Bou Saada, long known as the 'City of Happiness', is the classic gateway between the High Plateaus and the Sahara. Over two days you visit its natural waterfall and the historic Ferrero Mill, wander lively craft markets, share a Bedouin-style dinner with live music, and see the Sufi zawiya of El Hamel. It's an easy, atmospheric taste of southern Algeria, a few hours from Algiers.

Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 1 Algiers to Bou Saada

    Morning departure from Algiers by tourist vehicle, hotel check-in, then an afternoon at the natural waterfall, the historic Ferrero Mill and the traditional markets, with a Bedouin-style dinner and local music in the evening.

    • The Bou Saada waterfall — A natural cascade in the oasis — the cool green corner of the 'City of Happiness'.
    • The Ferrero Mill — A historic riverside mill, a landmark of old Bou Saada.
    • The traditional markets — Souks for silver jewellery, wool and dates, with a Bedouin dinner and music in the evening.

    A morning drive from Algiers, then the afternoon in town.

  2. Day 2 El Hamel Zawiya & return

    After breakfast, visit the mountain Sufi shrine of El Hamel with time for photos and the landscape, a local lunch in Bou Saada, then the afternoon return to Algiers.

    • The El Hamel zawiya — A hilltop Rahmaniyya Sufi shrine and Quranic school above Bou Saada — calm, austere and panoramic.

    A local lunch in Bou Saada, then the afternoon return to Algiers.
