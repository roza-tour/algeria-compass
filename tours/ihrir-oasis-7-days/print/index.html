Ihrir & the Tassili — 7 Days of Oases and Ergs

7 days · 6 nights · From €920 per person

Everyone expects the Sahara to be dry. Ihrir is where it is not — a sunken oasis of standing water, reeds and rock pools, an hour from dunes that run to the horizon. Private trip on your own dates, with a licensed local guide.

Day by day

Day 1Arrival in Djanet

Land at Djanet, the gateway to the Tassili n’Ajjer, and be met by our team. Transfer to the guesthouse, time to rest after the flight, and a convivial dinner before the circuit begins.

  • Tiska Airport — Met on arrival at Algeria’s deepest-south airport, 30 km from the oasis.
  • Djanet — Tuareg oasis capital of the south-east — palm groves beneath red cliffs.
  • The guesthouse in Djanet — A first night indoors — a courtyard house among the palms, and dinner with the team you will travel with all week.

Day 2Djanet → Ihrir → Idaren → Tasourt

Breakfast, then out by 4×4 to the valley of Ihrir — an enclosed oasis famous for its natural gueltas and its unexpectedly luxuriant vegetation. On to Idaren and Tasourt to explore rock engravings and more natural pools. Camp goes up, dinner is cooked on the fire, and you sleep under tent or under the stars.

  • Ihrir — A sunken oasis whose rock pools hold water all year.
  • The gueltas of Ihrir — Permanent rock pools along the canyon floor, with reeds, fish and birdlife in the middle of the desert.
  • Idaren & Tasourt — Rock engravings and natural basins along the valley.

Day 3Ihrir → Tilalin → Erg Admer

Breakfast at camp and a walk through the Tilalin area, then the road to Erg Admer. Climb the dunes for sunset over the erg, and camp in the middle of the desert.

  • Tilalin — A morning walk through the area’s rock formations and dry watercourses before the drive on.
  • Erg Admer — A great dune field — the climb up for sunset is the day’s reward.
  • The camp in the erg — Camp pitched in open sand with nothing at all on the horizon — the emptiest night of the circuit.

Day 4Erg Admer → Oued Essendilène → Tikoubaouine

Off to Oued Essendilène and a walk through its canyon, where swimming is possible depending on the season. Picnic lunch, then on to Tikoubaouine and its rock formations sculpted by the wind. Camp among the rocks.

  • Oued Essendilène — A canyon with permanent water — swimming in season.
  • The canyon walk — An hour on foot up the gorge to the water, between walls that close in as you go.
  • Tikoubaouine — Towers and arches carved by wind rather than water.

Day 5In Imouroudene → Adaik

Exploration of the In Imouroudene plateau, a panoramic walk and a picnic lunch. On to Adaik — known locally as the "little Sefar" for its concentration of rock art — with free time to take in the sunset before dinner at the bivouac.

  • In Imouroudene — A plateau with long views over the Tassili.
  • Adaik — The "little Sefar" — rock art without the plateau trek.
  • Sunset at Adaik — Free time on the rocks for the last light, before dinner at the bivouac.

Day 6Adaik → Timghas

Breakfast and the drive to Timghas, with an exploration walk and time spent on the Saharan flora that survives out here. Picnic lunch, camp pitched, and a last dinner under the stars.

  • Timghas — The final camp, and a closer look at what actually grows in the Sahara.
  • Saharan flora on foot — A walk given to what actually grows out here — acacia, tamarisk, calotropis and the plants the Tuareg still use.
  • The last bivouac — A final dinner over the fire under the whole sky, before the return to Djanet.

Day 7Timghas → Tigharghart → Djanet → home

Back towards Tigharghart for the "Crying Cow" engraving and the Elephant Rock, then return to Djanet and the transfer to the airport for the flight home.

  • Tigharghart — the "Crying Cow" — The Sahara’s most photographed engraving, on the way back in to the oasis.
  • The Elephant Rock — Tigharghart’s second famous engraving, a short walk from the first.
  • Djanet and Tiska Airport — Back to the oasis, then the transfer for the flight north.
