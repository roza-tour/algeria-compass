Overview

The full sweep of Algeria in ten days. From Algiers and the seaside Roman ruins of Tipaza, you trace the country's Roman heartland — Djémila and Timgad — cross to Constantine's gorge and bridges, and finish deep in the Sahara at Ghardaïa's M'Zab Valley. Four UNESCO sites, the capital, the highlands and the desert, paced to take it all in without rushing.

Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 1 Arrival in Algiers

    Airport welcome, hotel check-in and a welcome dinner.

  2. Day 2 Tipaza & Cherchell

    A coastal day at the Roman ruins of Tipaza — amphitheatre and mausoleum — with the Cherchell museum and sea views.

  3. Day 3 Algiers historical tour

    The Casbah, the Martyrs' Memorial and the National Army Museum.

  4. Day 4 Travel to Sétif

    Transfer east and visit the UNESCO Roman ruins of Djémila.

  5. Day 5 Constantine

    City tour of Constantine — its suspension bridges, mosque and palace.

  6. Day 6 Batna & Timgad

    Travel to Batna and explore the UNESCO Roman city of Timgad.

  7. Day 7 Journey to Ghardaïa

    Travel south to the M'Zab Valley and check in at a guesthouse.

  8. Day 8 Ghardaïa cultural tour

    The fortified villages, traditional mosques, souks and local cuisine of the M'Zab.

  9. Day 9 Return to Algiers

    Travel back to the capital, with the Hamma botanical garden, the Bardo Museum and Notre-Dame d'Afrique.

  10. Day 10 Departure

    Breakfast and transfer to the airport.
