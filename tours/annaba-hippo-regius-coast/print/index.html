Private tour itinerary

Annaba — Hippo Regius & the Edough Coast (3 Days)

3 days · 2 nights · From €324 per person

Saint Augustine's city, a basilica above the bay, mountain forests and turquoise coves — the north-east coast in three unhurried days. Private trip on your own dates, with a licensed local guide.

Day by day

Day 1Hippo Regius & Saint Augustine

The Basilica of Saint Augustine on its hill, the ruins of Hippo Regius below it, then the old town and the evening on the Cours de la Révolution.

  • The Basilica of Saint Augustine — The white basilica built on the hill above the ruins, with its view over the city and the bay.
  • Hippo Regius — The forum, baths, villas and Christian basilica of the Roman city where Augustine was bishop, and its museum of mosaics.
  • The old town & the Cours de la Révolution — The old quarter around the Mosque of Abu Marwan, then the city's great promenade in the evening.

Day 2The Edough & Cap de Garde

Up through the cork-oak forests of the Edough to the mountain village of Seraïdi, then down to the cliffs of Cap de Garde and the corniche at sunset.

  • The Edough massif — A winding road through cork oaks and pines, with an easy forest walk.
  • Seraïdi — The mountain village high above Annaba, with views over the whole bay.
  • Cap de Garde & the corniche — The lighthouse on its cliffs, then the coast road back to the city as the sun goes down.

Day 3The coves of the coast

West along the coast to the rocky coves and bays, with time to walk the shore or swim in season, then the transfer to Annaba airport.

  • The western coves — Small bays of rock and pine with clear turquoise water.
  • A coastal walk — An easy path along the shore, with stops for photographs.
  • Departure — Transfer to Annaba airport.
