Tipaza & Cherchell — Private Roman Day Tour

A private day on the Roman coast — the seaside ruins of Tipaza and the museum of ancient Caesarea, entirely at your own pace.

Overview

Tipaza is one of the most beautiful ancient sites in the Mediterranean, its Roman forum, baths and houses spread along the sea about 70 km west of Algiers. On this private day you explore the UNESCO ruins at your own pace, then visit the archaeological museum at Cherchell — Roman Caesarea — rich in statuary and mosaics. With a personal guide and hotel pickup, it's the relaxed way to see the Roman coast.

Highlights

  • Forum, baths and residential quarters
  • The Cherchell Archaeological Museum
  • A private, personalised pace
  • A walk through Cherchell's old harbour

Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 1 Tipaza ruins & Cherchell museum

    Hotel pickup and a private drive to Tipaza for a guided tour of its forum, baths and ancient quarters, then on to Cherchell — ancient Caesarea — for its museum of Roman statues and mosaics and a short walk in the old town, before the return transfer.

Are entrance fees included?
