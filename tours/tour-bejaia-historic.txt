## Overview

Béjaïa is where the green Djurdjura mountains drop straight into a deep-blue Mediterranean. In a day from Algiers you visit its historic fortress and museum, drive the dramatic Cap Carbon corniche above the cliffs, and unwind on the sand at Les Aiguades, with a traditional lunch by the water. It is the easiest way to taste the Kabyle coast — history, scenery and sea in one relaxed loop.

## Day by day

### Day 1 — Béjaïa fortress, Cap Carbon & the coast

Pickup from Algiers, a guided visit of the historic fortress and its museum, a walk along the Cap Carbon corniche, beach time at Les Aiguades, and a traditional lunch before the return transfer.
- **Fort Gouraya and its museum**: The fortress high above Béjaïa, with the bay, the port and the Kabyle mountains laid out below.
- **Cap Carbon**: The cape and its lighthouse set on a natural rock arch — among the highest sea lighthouses in the world.
- **Les Aiguades**: A cove of clear water below the corniche, with time on the beach before a traditional lunch.
