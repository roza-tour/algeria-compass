## Overview

Timimoun, the 'Red Oasis', is the heart of the Gourara — a Saharan world of ochre ksour, vast palm groves and the ingenious foggara channels that carry water for miles underground. Over five days you explore Charouine and its ksour, the oases of Aghlad and Ouled Saïd, ride a 4×4 across the great dunes, and share a traditional 'Merdoum' dinner under the stars, with evenings of local music. It's one of Algeria's most atmospheric and least-visited corners.

## Day by day

### Day 1 — Arrival in Timimoun

Welcome at Timimoun and transfer to the Ti Massin guesthouse, with time to settle in before a traditional dinner and overnight stay.
- **Timimoun**: The "red oasis" of the Gourara — an entire town built in red-ochre Sudanese-style earth architecture above a salt lake.
- **The Ti Massin guesthouse**: A traditional red-earth house of courtyards and terraces, and the base for the whole stay.
- **The escarpment at sunset**: The ledge above the sebkha where Timimoun’s walls turn deep orange in the last light, before a traditional dinner.

### Day 2 — Charouine — ksour & palm groves

An excursion to Charouine for Ksar Guentour and its palm grove, an open-air lunch, and the ksar and oasis at Tala, returning for a dune sunset and a festive evening with music.
- **Ksar Guentour & its palm grove**: A red-mud ksar above its palmery — classic Gourara architecture.
- **The ksar & oasis at Tala**: Another oasis of earthen towers among the palms.
- **Dune sunset & music evening**: Sand at dusk and an evening of local Gourara music.

### Day 3 — Aghlad & Ouled Saïd — foggaras

Visit the gardens and ancient foggara channels of Ouled Saïd, a palm-grove picnic with tea at Beni Aïssi and its ksar, and sunset over the large dunes.
- **The Ouled Saïd foggaras**: Ancient underground irrigation galleries threading water to the gardens — Saharan ingenuity at its finest.
- **Beni Aïssi & its ksar**: A palm-grove picnic and tea beside an old ksar.
- **The large dunes**: Sunset over the high sand.

### Day 4 — Desert adventure & star dinner

The Ighzer site in the morning, then an afternoon 4x4 safari across golden dunes to a panoramic sunset, followed by a desert-camp 'Merdoum' dinner under the stars.
- **The Ighzer site**: A desert site explored in the cool of the morning.
- **4×4 dune safari**: Golden dunes to a panoramic sunset.
- **A 'Merdoum' camp dinner**: A traditional sand-pit-roasted Merdoum feast under the stars.

### Day 5 — Market & departure

Visit Timimoun's traditional market for spices and handicrafts, then the return transfer toward Algiers.
- **Timimoun's market**: The red town's market for spices and handicrafts.
- **The old ksar quarter**: A last walk through the red-earth lanes and the arcaded covered passages of the old town.
- **The road north**: The return transfer toward Algiers along the northern edge of the Grand Erg Occidental.

## Questions

**Where is Timimoun?**
In the Gourara region of the central Algerian Sahara; reached by domestic flight.

**What is a foggara?**
An ancestral underground channel system that brings water to the oases — you'll see how it works.

**Is accommodation included?**

**What is the 'Merdoum' dinner?**
