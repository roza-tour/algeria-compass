Ghardaïa — 3-Day M'Zab Valley Tour

Three days in the M'Zab Valley — the fortified hill-towns of Ghardaïa and Beni Isguen, the ancient foggara water system, palm oases, and a 4×4 desert evening under the stars.

Highlights

  • The mud-brick hill-town of Ghardaïa in the M'Zab Valley
  • The fortified village of Beni Isguen
  • The ancestral 'foggara' water-sharing system
  • Palm oases and traditional crafts
  • A 4×4 desert safari toward El Guerrara
  • Dinner under the desert stars

Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 1 Ghardaïa & the oasis

    Met at Ghardaïa airport and taken to your accommodation. Explore the hill-town's narrow alleys and lively market, lunch in a restored heritage house, then a palm oasis and the foggara channels. Dinner and overnight in Ghardaïa.

  2. Day 2 Beni Isguen & desert safari

    Morning in the fortified village of Beni Isguen and the Tafilelt eco-town, with panoramic valley views. After a traditional lunch, a 4×4 safari toward El Guerrara and dinner in the open desert under the stars.

  3. Day 3 Departure

    A relaxed breakfast, then transfer back to the airport — the end of a journey through Algeria's Saharan heart.
