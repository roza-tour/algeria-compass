## Overview

Algiers wears its history on the hillside. In a single day you climb through the UNESCO-listed Casbah — the dense Ottoman medina of stepped lanes, courtyard houses and old mosques above the bay — take in the archaeology and art of the Bardo National Museum, and stand beneath the soaring Martyrs' Memorial for the city's best view over the white houses and the Mediterranean. A traditional lunch breaks the day. It is the ideal first day in Algeria: the capital's layers, read end to end with a local guide.

## Day by day

### Day 1 — Casbah, Bardo Museum & Martyrs' Memorial

A guided full-day loop through the old Casbah quarter, the collections of the Bardo National Museum, and the Martyrs' Memorial, with a traditional lunch at a local restaurant between visits.
- **The Casbah of Algiers**: The UNESCO-listed Ottoman citadel — a steep white labyrinth of palaces, mosques and courtyard houses climbing above the bay.
- **The Bardo National Museum**: Prehistory and ethnography inside a graceful Moorish villa, from Saharan rock-art casts to silver jewellery and everyday Algeria.
- **The Martyrs’ Memorial (Maqam Echahid)**: Three soaring concrete palm-fronds raised in 1982 over the city, honouring the dead of the war of independence.
