Private tour itinerary

Taghit & the Saoura — Dunes of the Grand Erg (4 Days)

4 days · 3 nights · From €394 per person

An earthen ksar, a ribbon of palms and the highest dunes of the western Sahara rising straight behind them. Private trip on your own dates, with a licensed local guide.

Day by day

Day 1Béchar to Taghit

Arrival at Béchar airport and the road to Taghit through the stony hamada, the first view of the dunes, and the evening in the oasis.

  • Béchar airport — Welcome by our team and the drive south-west to Taghit.
  • The hamada — The stony plateau of the Saoura, until the dunes appear on the horizon.
  • Taghit at dusk — The first evening at the guesthouse, at the edge of the sand.

Day 2The ksar and the palm grove

The old ksar of Taghit and its lanes, the palm grove and gardens along the Oued Zousfana, the rock engravings near the oasis, and sunset from the great dune.

  • The ksar of Taghit — Earthen lanes, covered passages and the view from the top of the old village.
  • The palm grove — Date palms and gardens watered by the Oued Zousfana.
  • The great dune — A climb at your own pace for the sunset over the erg and the oasis.

Day 3A day in the Grand Erg

By 4x4 into the dunes of the Grand Erg Occidental, with walks in the sand, tea and lunch in the shade, and the last light over the dunes.

  • Into the erg — Tracks between the dunes, far from the oasis.
  • Tea in the sand — Lunch and tea prepared by the team in the shade.
  • The last light — Sunset over the sand sea before returning to Taghit.

Day 4Back to Béchar

A last walk in the oasis, then the road back to Béchar airport.

  • A morning in the oasis — A last stroll in the palm grove.
  • The road back — Across the hamada to Béchar.
  • Departure — Transfer to Béchar airport.
