## Overview

Biskra, the Queen of the Zibans, sits exactly where the mountains of the Aurès meet the desert. Its palm groves grow some of the finest dates in the world, and to the north the road enters El Kantara, the gorge the old travellers called the Mouth of the Sahara. Higher up, at Ghoufi, abandoned Chaoui villages hang on the cliffs of a canyon above a ribbon of palms, one of the most striking views in Algeria. To the south-east, Sidi Okba keeps one of the oldest mosques in North Africa. Three days, easy walks, and a part of the country few travellers ever see.

## Day by day

### Day 1 — Biskra, Queen of the Zibans

Arrival in Biskra, a walk in the palm groves and the Landon Garden, old Biskra, and the sunset at the edge of the desert.
- **The palm groves**: The Deglet Nour date palms of the Zibans, with their irrigation channels and gardens.
- **The Landon Garden**: A shaded garden of rare trees laid out in the nineteenth century in the heart of the oasis.
- **Sunset over the desert**: The evening light where the last hills meet the Sahara.

### Day 2 — El Kantara & the Ghoufi balconies

North through the gorge of El Kantara, then into the Aurès to the canyon of Ghoufi and its villages hanging above the palms.
- **The gorge of El Kantara**: The Mouth of the Sahara: a narrow cleft in the mountains, its palm grove and old village.
- **The Ghoufi balconies**: Chaoui villages of stone and earth on the cliffs of the Oued Abiod canyon.
- **Into the canyon**: An easy walk down to the gardens and palms along the river.

### Day 3 — Sidi Okba & the oases

The mosque of Sidi Okba, the oasis of Chetma, then the transfer to Biskra airport.
- **The mosque of Sidi Okba**: One of the oldest mosques in North Africa, around the tomb of Uqba ibn Nafi.
- **The oasis of Chetma**: Earthen lanes and gardens in the shade of the palms.
- **Departure**: Transfer to Biskra airport.
