Roman Algeria — Tipaza, Djémila & Timgad (3 Days)

Three days, three of the greatest Roman cities on Earth — by the sea at Tipaza, on the hillside at Djémila, and across the grid of Timgad.

Overview

Algeria holds some of the best-preserved Roman cities anywhere, and this journey strings together the finest. You begin on the coast at Tipaza, where temples and bathhouses meet the Mediterranean, and at Caesarea (Cherchell) with its museum of statuary and mosaics. You cross to Djémila, draped over its hillside with colonnaded streets, monumental gateways and mosaic floors, and finish at Timgad — the textbook grid city Trajan founded for his veterans, with its theatre, forum and famous library. Three UNESCO-calibre sites, almost without crowds.

Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 1 Tipaza & Cherchell

    The Roman streets, bathhouses, temples and necropolis of Tipaza, then Caesarea (Cherchell) and its archaeological museum.

  2. Day 2 Djémila (Cuicul)

    Djémila's colonnaded streets, monumental gateways, Roman theatre, religious complexes and mosaic-floored houses, with the local museum.

  3. Day 3 Timgad

    The Roman theatre, forum and public library of Timgad, its famous grid plan, baths and temples.

    Tour FAQ

    Are these sites UNESCO-listed?

    Tipaza, Djémila and Timgad are all UNESCO World Heritage Sites; Cherchell (Caesarea) is the related Roman capital.

    How much driving is involved?

    It is a touring itinerary across northern Algeria, with scenic road transfers between the sites.

    Is it suited to keen history travellers?

    Ideal — it is one of the most complete Roman itineraries in North Africa.

    When is the best time to go?

    Spring and autumn; the sites are open and exposed, so summer midday heat is best avoided.
