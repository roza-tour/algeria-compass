Roman Algeria — Tipaza, Djémila & Timgad (3 Days)

3 days · 2 nights · From €384 per person

Three days, three of the greatest Roman cities on Earth — by the sea at Tipaza, on the hillside at Djémila, and across the grid of Timgad. Private trip on your own dates, with a licensed local guide.

Day by day

Day 1Tipaza & Cherchell

The Roman streets, bathhouses, temples and necropolis of Tipaza, then Caesarea (Cherchell) and its archaeological museum.

  • Roman Tipaza — A UNESCO-listed Roman port strung along the Mediterranean — basilicas, baths and a theatre with the waves at their feet.
  • Cherchell (ancient Caesarea) — Juba II's old capital, whose museum holds some of Algeria's finest Roman and Greek mosaics and statuary.
  • The Royal Mausoleum of Mauretania — A great stone drum on a hill above the sea — the 1st-century-BC tomb of Berber king Juba II and Cleopatra Selene, daughter of Cleopatra of Egypt.

Day 2Djémila (Cuicul)

Djémila's colonnaded streets, monumental gateways, Roman theatre, religious complexes and mosaic-floored houses, with the local museum.

  • Djémila’s colonnaded streets — Monumental gateways and paved avenues of the Roman city in its mountain bowl.
  • The Djémila theatre — A steep theatre set into the hillside, still commanding the valley.
  • The Djémila mosaics — On-site mosaics among the richest in the Roman world, in the site museum.

Day 3Timgad

The Roman theatre, forum and public library of Timgad, its famous grid plan, baths and temples.

  • Trajan's Arch — The triumphal arch framing Timgad's main street — the city's iconic silhouette.
  • The Timgad library & theatre — The ruins of a rare Roman public library and a 3,500-seat theatre still in use today.
  • Timgad’s grid, baths & temples — The textbook Roman plan that gives Timgad its fame.

Good to know

  • Best season: Spring & autumn — comfortable on exposed sites
  • Pace: Moderate — walking on uneven ancient ground
  • Style: Private, guided, 2 nights
  • Bring: Sun protection, water, sturdy shoes, a hat
