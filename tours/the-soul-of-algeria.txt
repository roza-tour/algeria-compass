You take in the Bardo Museum, Notre-Dame d'Afrique, the Martyrs' Memorial and the UNESCO Casbah in Algiers; the seaside Roman ruins of Tipaza and the Cherchell museum; and the oasis town of Bou Saada on the Saharan edge. ## Day by day

### Day 1 — Algiers

The Bardo Museum and its mosaics, Notre-Dame d'Afrique above the bay, and the Martyrs' Memorial.
- **The Bardo Museum**: Prehistory and ethnography inside a graceful Moorish villa, from Saharan rock-art casts to silver jewellery and everyday Algeria.
- **Notre-Dame d'Afrique**: A 19th-century basilica — 'Our Lady of Africa' — on the cliffs above the bay, inscribed for Muslims and Christians alike.
- **The Martyrs' Memorial (Maqam Echahid)**: Three soaring concrete palm-fronds raised in 1982 over the city, honouring the dead of the war of independence.

### Day 2 — Tipaza & Cherchell

The Royal Mausoleum of Mauretania, the Roman archaeological park of Tipaza, and the Cherchell museum.
- **The Royal Mausoleum of Mauretania**: A great stone drum on a hill above the sea — the 1st-century-BC tomb of Berber king Juba II and Cleopatra Selene, daughter of Cleopatra of Egypt.
- **Roman Tipaza**: A UNESCO-listed Roman port strung along the Mediterranean — basilicas, baths and a theatre with the waves at their feet.
- **Cherchell (ancient Caesarea)**: Juba II's old capital, whose museum holds some of Algeria's finest Roman and Greek mosaics and statuary.

### Day 3 — Bou Saada

South to Bou Saada for its fortress and panoramas, the town museum of crafts and relics, and its historic mosque.
- **Bou Saada panoramas**: The fortress and viewpoints over the oasis town.
- **The Nasreddine Dinet Museum**: The museum of the Orientalist painter who made Bou Saada famous, with his works and relics.
- **The historic mosque**: The old town mosque at the heart of Bou Saada.

### Day 4 — Algiers

Back to Algiers for the UNESCO Casbah, the Hamma botanical garden, and the Museum of the Mujahideen.
- **The Casbah of Algiers**: The UNESCO-listed Ottoman citadel — a steep white labyrinth of palaces, mosques and courtyard houses climbing above the bay.
- **The Hamma Botanical Garden**: The grand 19th-century Jardin d'Essai, one of the finest botanical gardens in Africa.
- **The Museum of the Mujahideen**: The story of the independence fighters, told beneath the Martyrs’ Memorial.
