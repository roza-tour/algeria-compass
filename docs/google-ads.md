# Google Ads — copy & paste

Search campaigns, one per language. Each block below is ready to paste into Google Ads → **Campaigns → + New campaign → Search**.

Every headline is ≤ 30 characters, every description ≤ 90, every sitelink ≤ 25 / 35, every callout ≤ 25 — checked by script, so Google will accept all of them as written. The number in brackets is the character count; **do not paste it**.


## Before you start (once)

1. **Conversion tracking** — send me your Google Ads conversion ID (AW-…) and I will install it. Without it Google cannot optimise for bookings.
2. **Bidding** — start with *Maximise clicks* and a max CPC of about €1–1.50 for the first 2 weeks; once 15–30 conversions are recorded, switch to *Maximise conversions*.
3. **Location setting** — choose *Presence: people in or regularly in your targeted locations* (not "interest"), and **exclude Algeria**.
4. **Networks** — untick *Display Network* for these search campaigns.
5. **Budget** — start small (e.g. €10–15/day per language) and move money to the language that books.
6. **Keyword match types** — paste keywords exactly as written: `"…"` = phrase match, `[…]` = exact match.

## Rules that keep these ads approved
- Every promise in these ads (72-hour hold, visa-refusal refund, locked price, 5% early booking, free cancellation) is written on the landing page and in /booking-terms/ — keep them true, or change BOOKING in `src/config.ts` and these ads together.
- No prices are quoted in the headlines on purpose: the planner shows the price after five questions, and Google rejects ads whose price is not visible straight away on the landing page.
- Do not add exclamation marks to headlines; Google disapproves them.
