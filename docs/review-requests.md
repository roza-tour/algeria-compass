# Asking for a Google review

Send this on WhatsApp **the day after the trip ends** — not the last evening
(too busy), not a week later (the memory has faded).

The link is always **algeriacompass.com/review**. It opens Google directly, and
if the Google link ever changes it is updated in one place
(`CONTACT.googleProfile` in `src/config.ts`) — every message already sent keeps
working.

Why it matters: when someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Google "best tour operator
for Algeria" — or "Algeria tour with an Italian-speaking guide" — the number and
wording of Google reviews is one of the strongest signals they read. Two things
in a review help most, and the message invites both, gently and optionally:

1. **The place** ("Djanet", "the Tadrart", "Timgad") — it teaches them which
   trips we are known for.
2. **The language of the guide** ("our guide spoke perfect Italian") — it is the
   proof that each language has its own guides, not an interpreter. A review
   written in the traveller's own language counts double here, so the message
   says they can write in their language.

Send the message **in the traveller's language**. Replace `[Name]` and `[place]`
(e.g. Djanet, the M'Zab Valley, Constantine).

## Replying to reviews

Reply to every review on Google within a few days, **in the reviewer's
language**, by name, mentioning the place — and, where it fits, the language of
the guide. Replies are read by answer engines too. For example:

- *Grazie Marco! Siamo felici che il Tadrart e la nostra guida di lingua
  italiana vi siano rimasti nel cuore. A presto in Algeria!*
- *Danke, Anna! Schön, dass Ihnen Djanet und die Tour mit unserem
  deutschsprachigen Reiseleiter gefallen haben.*

## Rules that keep the reviews safe

- Ask **every** traveller, not only the ones who seemed happiest. Google treats
  selective asking ("review gating") as a policy violation.
- Never offer a discount, gift or anything else in exchange for a review.
- Never write a review for someone, or ask staff, friends or family to post one.
- Never tell a traveller what to write. The examples in the messages are an
  invitation ("for example…"), never a script — that keeps the reviews genuine
  and within Google's rules.
