# Asking for a Google review

Send this on WhatsApp **the day after the trip ends** — not the last evening
(too busy), not a week later (the memory has faded).

The link is always **algeriacompass.com/review**. It opens Google directly, and
if the Google link ever changes it is updated in one place
(`CONTACT.googleProfile` in `src/config.ts`) — every message already sent keeps
working.

Why it matters: when someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Google "best tour operator
for Algeria", the number and wording of Google reviews is one of the strongest
signals they read. A review that **names the place** ("Djanet", "the Tadrart",
"Timgad") also teaches them which trips we are known for — so the message asks
for that, gently.

Replace `[Name]` and `[place]` (e.g. Djanet, the M'Zab Valley, Constantine).

Hi [Name], it was a real pleasure having you with us in [place]. If you have two
minutes, a few words on Google about the trip — where you went, what stayed with
you — would mean a lot to a small Algerian company:
algeriacompass.com/review
Thank you, and safe travels home!

## Rules that keep the reviews safe

- Ask **every** traveller, not only the ones who seemed happiest. Google treats
  selective asking ("review gating") as a policy violation.
- Never offer a discount, gift or anything else in exchange for a review.
- Never write a review for someone, or ask staff, friends or family to post one.
- Reply to every review on Google, good or bad, within a few days — briefly, by
  name, mentioning the place. Replies are read by answer engines too.
