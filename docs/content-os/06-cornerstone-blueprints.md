## 1. Complete Algeria Travel Guide  (the master pillar)
- **Audience:** first-time visitor planning a trip; researchers scoping the country.
- **Intent:** informational + commercial-investigation ("Algeria travel guide", "visit Algeria").
- **Keywords:** Algeria travel guide, visiting Algeria, Algeria trip, things to do in Algeria.
- **Entities:** country, 7 regions, top provinces, the 7 UNESCO sites, seasons, transport.
- **Sections:** why Algeria · is it safe/visa (summarise → link pillars) · when to go · regions overview · top experiences · sample itineraries (1wk/2wk) · costs · getting around · responsible travel · FAQs.
- **Visual:** hero (Sahara or Casbah) + region thumbnails + 1 map graphic.
- **Schema:** Article + ItemList (regions) + FAQPage. **Links:** ↓ to all 16 pillars (this is the hub of hubs).

## 2. Algeria Visa Guide
- **Audience:** anyone needing entry rules. **Intent:** transactional ("Algeria visa requirements 2026").
- **Entities:** visa/travel-planning theme, country, transport (entry points).
- **Sections:** direct answer (who needs one) · types · application steps (HowTo) · invitation/voucher reality · visa-on-arrival for organised south · e-visa status · common rejections · official links · FAQs.
- **Sources:** Algerian MFA/consulate + a reputable summary; **dated**. **Schema:** Article + HowTo + FAQPage. **Update: 6 mo.**

## 3. Algeria Safety Guide
- **Intent:** reassurance/transactional ("is Algeria safe 2026").
- **Sections:** direct verdict · areas to know (north vs deep south/border zones) · solo & women travellers · desert/permits · health · scams/petty crime · emergency numbers · official advisories · FAQs.
- **Sources:** government travel advisories (named, dated). **Update: 6 mo.** Tone: calm, factual, no fear-mongering or false guarantees.

## 4. Algeria Transportation Guide
- **Sections:** options overview · air (Air Algérie, airports) · SNTF rail · intercity bus · louage shared taxis · Algiers metro/tram · driving & 4x4 in the south · costs table · accessibility · FAQs.
- **AI block:** mode comparison + cost table. **Update 12 mo.**

## 5. Algeria Food Guide
- **Sections:** food culture · couscous (UNESCO ICH) · regional dishes (east/west/south) · street food · pastries · tea & hospitality · vegetarian · where to eat · FAQs.
- **Entities:** food×13, regions, tradition (tea-hospitality). **AI block:** dish×region table.

## 6. Algeria UNESCO Guide
- **Sections:** overview + map · one block per site (×7: what/why/visiting) · how to combine · conservation · FAQs.
- **Sources:** **the 7 UNESCO list URLs are mandatory.** **Entities:** unesco×7 + provinces + periods. **AI block:** Quick Facts per site.

## 7. Algeria Sahara Guide
- **Audience:** desert-curious, adventure & photography travellers. **Intent:** awe + high purchase intent.
- **Sections:** what the Algerian Sahara is · regions (Tassili, Tadrart, Hoggar, ergs, oases) · what to do · best time (monthly table) · getting there (Djanet/Tamanrasset) · permits & guides · safety · packing · responsible desert travel · FAQs.
- **Entities:** sahara-area×7, Tassili/Tadrart/Hoggar, Djanet, transport (4x4). **Visual:** strongest gallery on the site.

## 8. Algeria Road Trip Guide — routes (Roman NE loop, coast, M'Zab/oasis, Saoura), driving realities, fuel/distances, 4x4 vs 2WD, sample multi-day routes, costs table, FAQs.
## 9. Algeria Photography Guide — best locations by region, light/season, gear, drone/permission rules, cultural etiquette, sample shot-list, FAQs.
## 10. Algeria Culture Guide — peoples (Amazigh/Arab) & languages, music (raï/Andalusian), crafts, festivals, religion & etiquette, how to experience respectfully, FAQs. Sources ≥4.
## 11. Algeria History Guide — Timeline-led: prehistory→Numidia→Rome→Islam→dynasties→Ottoman→colonial→independence; where to see each today; FAQs. Sources ≥5.
## 12. Algeria Luxury Travel Guide — what premium looks like in Algeria (private/guided, best stays, chartered desert), sample high-end itinerary, FAQs. Honest (no fake 5-star claims).
## 13. Algeria Family Travel Guide — coast, easy Roman sites, oasis day-trips, practicalities with kids, safety, FAQs.
## 14. Algeria Adventure Travel Guide — Sahara expeditions, Hoggar/Djurdjura trekking, Aurès canyons, operators & permits, fitness/seasons, FAQs.
