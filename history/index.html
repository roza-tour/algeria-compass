History of Algeria

From Numidia and Roman Africa through the Islamic dynasties, Ottoman regency and independence — a short history for travellers.

Home › History of Algeria
Quick answer

Algeria's history reaches back to the Berber kingdom of Numidia and the Roman province of Africa, whose cities — Djémila, Timgad, Tipaza — are among the best preserved anywhere. Successive Islamic dynasties, the Ottoman regency of Algiers, 132 years of French colonisation and the 1954–62 war of independence shaped the modern nation.
