Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Algeria?

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are best countrywide; winter is the prime season for the deep Sahara, while summer suits the coast.

How do travellers get around Algeria?

Domestic flights link the north with Saharan towns, trains and coaches connect northern cities, and 4x4s with guides are used in the desert.
