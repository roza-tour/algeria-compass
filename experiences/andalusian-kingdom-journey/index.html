The Andalusian Kingdom Journey

Five days through Algeria's Hispano-Moorish west — Tlemcen, Oran and the legacy of Muslim Spain.

Quick answer

The Andalusian Kingdom Journey is a 5-day private tour of western Algeria — Tlemcen and Oran — exploring the region's Hispano-Moorish architecture, history and classical Andalusian music.

From USD 720 per person · 5 days

Highlights

  • The Great Mosque of Tlemcen and Mansourah
  • Sidi Boumediene's Hispano-Moorish art
  • Oran's coast, music and history
  • Classical Andalusian music heritage

Day by day

Day 1 — Arrive Oran

The vibrant western port; evening on the coast.

Days 2–3 — Tlemcen

Great Mosque, Mansourah, Sidi Boumediene, El Mechouar.

Day 4 — West coast

Mediterranean towns and cuisine.

Day 5 — Departure

Final morning, onward travel.

For lovers of art, architecture and music: the refined western face of Algeria, where Andalusia lives on.

Frequently asked questions

Why western Algeria?

The west, especially Tlemcen, preserves Algeria's richest Andalusian heritage — the culture carried from Muslim Spain.

Is Oran included?

Yes — the journey begins in Oran, Algeria's lively second city and a centre of raï music.
