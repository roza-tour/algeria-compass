City of Bridges Expedition

Four days through Algeria's dramatic north-east — Constantine's gorge, Ottoman palaces and Roman echoes.

Quick answer

The City of Bridges Expedition is a 4-day private tour of Constantine and Algeria's north-east, taking in the gorge and suspension bridges, the Palace of Ahmed Bey, and the surrounding Roman heritage.

From USD 540 per person · 4 days

Highlights

  • Constantine's gorge and suspension bridges
  • The Palace of Ahmed Bey
  • Old medina and Ottoman quarters
  • Day trip toward the Roman north-east

Day by day

Day 1 — Arrive Constantine

Gorge viewpoints at golden hour.

Day 2 — The bridges & palace

Sidi M'Cid bridge, Ahmed Bey Palace, medina.

Day 3 — Roman north-east

Excursion to nearby ancient sites.

Day 4 — Departure

Final views and onward travel.

A short, dramatic introduction to eastern Algeria, anchored by one of the most spectacularly sited cities on earth.
