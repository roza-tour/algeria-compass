The Andalusian Kingdom Journey

The Andalusian Kingdom Journey is a 5-day private tour of western Algeria — Tlemcen and Oran — exploring the region's Hispano-Moorish architecture, history and classical Andalusian music.
sahara

Bou Saada Oasis Escape

The Bou Saada Oasis Escape is a two-day private trip to Bou Saada, an oasis town on the edge of the Algerian Sahara in M'Sila state, taking in its palm grove, old ksar, the Moulin Ferrero gorge and the legacy of the painter Nasreddine Dinet.
highlands

City of Bridges Expedition

The City of Bridges Expedition is a 4-day private tour of Constantine and Algeria's north-east, taking in the gorge and suspension bridges, the Palace of Ahmed Bey, and the surrounding Roman heritage.
sahara

The Tassili Odyssey

The Tassili Odyssey is an 8-day private Sahara expedition from Djanet, combining the UNESCO rock-art plateau of Tassili n'Ajjer with the red dunes of the Tadrart Rouge, led by Tuareg guides.
