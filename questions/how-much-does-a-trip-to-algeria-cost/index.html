Cost

How much does a trip to Algeria cost?

Written by Algeria Compass Editorial Team Reviewed by Algeria Compass Editorial Review Updated June 7, 2026
Quick answer

Algeria is affordable by European standards. Our private, guided day tours start from €30 per person, and multi-day private journeys typically run from about €200 to €1,200 depending on length, region and the season. On-the-ground costs — food, local transport and everyday items — are modest.

Algeria is a good-value destination. With Algeria Compass, a private guided day tour starts from €30 per person, and multi-day private journeys run from roughly €200 to €1,200, depending on how many days, which regions, the level of accommodation and the time of year. You can see exact, per-tour pricing on each tour page.

Beyond the tour itself, day-to-day costs in Algeria are modest compared with Europe: meals, local transport and everyday items are inexpensive. The currency is the Algerian dinar (DZD). For a fuller breakdown of budgeting, see our complete Algeria travel guide.

Every quote we give is a fixed price for a defined itinerary, so there are no hidden extras — tell us your dates and we’ll price your trip exactly.
