Trust & transparency

Editorial standards

Algeria Compass is a travel publication about Algeria. Our value rests entirely on being accurate and trustworthy, so we are open about who creates our content, how it is researched and verified, and how we handle updates and corrections. Below are the people behind the work and the policies that govern it.

The people

Editorial team
Who researches and writes our guides.
Review & fact-checking
Who verifies information before and after publication.

Our policies

Editorial Policy
How Algeria Compass researches, writes, reviews and stands behind its Algeria travel content — and why our editorial work is independent of the sell.
Research Methodology
The step-by-step method we use to research Algeria travel content, the sources we prioritise, and how we separate fact from judgement.
Fact-Checking Policy
How we verify factual claims before publication, what counts as an acceptable source, and what happens to claims we cannot verify.
Corrections Policy
How to report an error, how we assess it, how we fix it, and how significant corrections are disclosed.
Content Review Process
The stages every page passes through from research to publication, who signs off, and the permissions at each stage.
Content Update Policy
How often we revisit published content, what triggers an update, and how updates are dated and distinguished from corrections.
Transparency Policy
What we disclose about who we are, how we make money, how we produce content, and how our interests are kept separate from our editorial work.
AI Usage Policy
An honest account of how and where we use AI tools, the human verification every claim passes through, and the things we never use AI to do.
Author Guidelines
The standards every writer for Algeria Compass works to: sourcing, tone, accuracy, attribution and the separation of fact from opinion.
Sources Policy
The kinds of sources we rely on, how we cite them, and how readers can trace the facts on our pages back to authoritative references.
