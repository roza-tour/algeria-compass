## Getting there
There are no direct flights, so plan on one or two connections into **Algiers (ALG)** — usually through Doha, Dubai or Istanbul. From Sydney or Melbourne the journey is typically 22 to 28 hours; many travellers break it with a stopover. Check current schedules when you book.

## The visa
Visitors from Australia need a visa for Algeria. There are two routes:

1. **A tourist visa from the Algerian embassy or consulate** that serves you, applied for before you travel. Consulates want an invitation letter and a confirmed programme from a licensed Algerian operator — we prepare both **free with every booking**.
2. **The Saharan visa-authorisation**, for organised trips that enter Algeria through the south, such as Djanet or Timimoun. We issue the authorisation (**€40**) and the visa sticker is issued on arrival; a separate sticker tax depends on your nationality.

Send us your nationality and travel dates and we will tell you which route applies. The details are on our [entry and e-Visa page](/evisa/) and in our [visa guide](/blog/algeria-visa-guide/).

## Time, power and money
- **Time:** Algeria runs on UTC+1 all year, with no daylight saving. **Sydney and Melbourne are 9 hours ahead** of Algiers in the Australian winter and 10 hours ahead during Australian daylight saving; **Perth is 7 hours ahead** all year.
- **Power:** Algeria uses 230 V power — the same voltage as Australia — but **European Type C and F plugs**, so an adapter is all you need.
- **Money:** Our tour prices are in euros, and you pay us before the trip. On the ground Algeria runs on cash: bring **euros** (or US dollars) in clean notes to change — Australian dollars are hard to change. Cards are rarely accepted outside big hotels — see [can I use credit cards in Algeria?](/questions/can-i-use-credit-cards-in-algeria/)

## When to go
Here the calendar works in your favour: the Sahara is travelled from **October to April**, which is exactly the Australian summer holiday season. **Christmas and New Year in the desert** are among the best weeks of the year — see our [winter guide](/blog/christmas-new-year-in-algeria/).
