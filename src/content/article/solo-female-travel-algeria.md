Is Algeria safe for women travelling alone? The honest answer is **generally yes, with common-sense precautions** — and many women travel the country solo without serious problems. Here's a straight guide, not a sugar-coated one.

## The overall picture
Algerians are, as a rule, hospitable and protective of guests. The **north and the usual tourist routes** — Algiers, Constantine, Oran, the Roman cities, the coast — are welcoming. As a foreign woman you may attract **attention** (looks, comments), and occasional **low-level harassment** can happen, as in much of the wider region. Serious incidents are uncommon.

## Dress and demeanour
- Dress **modestly**: cover shoulders and knees, keep a scarf handy.
- Project **confidence** and purpose when walking; it noticeably reduces unwanted attention.
- Be friendly but **discreet** about your accommodation and plans with strangers.

## Getting around
- Use **trusted transport** — official taxis, arranged transfers, trains between cities.
- After dark, stay in **busier, well-lit areas**.
- Learn a few words of **French or Arabic**; it changes interactions for the better.

## The Sahara
The deep south is a **guided experience for everyone**: licensed operator, guide and 4x4. Within that structure it's safe and well-organised — you don't travel the deep desert independently, male or female.
