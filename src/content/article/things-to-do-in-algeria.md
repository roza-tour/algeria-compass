Algeria is the largest country in Africa and one of its least-visited — which means its greatest sights come without the crowds. Here are the essential things to do, grouped by region so you can build them into a trip.

## The Roman north
Algeria has some of the **best-preserved Roman cities on earth**, and almost no queues.

1. **Timgad** — a complete Roman colonial town laid out on a grid, with its arch and theatre.
2. **Djémila** — spectacularly set in the hills, arguably the most beautiful Roman site in the country.
3. **Tipaza** — Roman ruins right on the Mediterranean, an easy day trip from Algiers.

## Algiers and the coast
4. **The Casbah of Algiers** (UNESCO) — the stepped Ottoman old town above the bay.
5. **The seafront and the Martyrs' Memorial** — the modern city's landmarks.
6. **The Mediterranean coast** — beaches and fishing towns east and west of the capital.

## The Sahara
The deep south is a **guided, winter expedition** — and the highlight of many trips.

7. **Tassili n'Ajjer** (near Djanet) — a plateau of prehistoric rock art and sandstone "forests" (UNESCO).
8. **The Tadrart** — red dunes and arches in the deep south-east.
9. **The Hoggar** (near Tamanrasset) — volcanic peaks and the Assekrem sunrise.
10. **Timimoun** — the "red oasis" and its ksour.

## The oasis towns and the west
11. **The M'Zab valley** (Ghardaïa) — five fortified oasis towns that influenced modern architecture (UNESCO).
12. **Tlemcen** — Almohad and Zianid heritage, the Andalusian heart of Algeria.
13. **Oran** — the raï-music city, with a lively coast and culture.
14. **Constantine** — the dramatic city of bridges and gorges.

## Culture, food and craft
15. **Algerian cuisine** — couscous, chorba, and regional specialities. See our [food guide](/blog/algerian-food-guide/).
16. **Traditional crafts** — carpets, pottery and silverwork. See our [handicrafts guide](/blog/algerian-handicrafts/).
17. **Beni Hammad Fort** — the first capital of the Hammadid dynasty (UNESCO).
18. **Berber (Amazigh) Kabylie** — mountains, villages and coast east of Algiers.
19. **Sétif and the eastern highlands** — Roman heritage and green plateaus.
20. **A hammam and a mint tea** — the everyday pleasures that make the trip.
