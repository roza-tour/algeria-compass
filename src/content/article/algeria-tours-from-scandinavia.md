If the northern winter is the problem, the Algerian Sahara is the answer that few people know about: real desert, real light, and almost nobody else in it.

This page gathers what matters when you are travelling from the Nordics: getting there, the visa, time and money, when to go, and which of our private tours fit best. Every tour is private, on your own dates, with a licensed local guide. All our guides speak English.

## Getting there
Plan on one connection into **Algiers (ALG)** from Copenhagen, Stockholm, Oslo or Helsinki — usually through Paris, Frankfurt, Amsterdam or Istanbul. Flying time is around 4 to 5 hours plus the connection. Check current schedules when you book.

## The visa
Visitors from Scandinavia and the Nordic countries need a visa for Algeria. There are two routes:

1. **A tourist visa from the Algerian embassy or consulate** that serves you, applied for before you travel. Consulates want an invitation letter and a confirmed programme from a licensed Algerian operator — we prepare both **free with every booking**.
2. **The Saharan visa-authorisation**, for organised trips that enter Algeria through the south, such as Djanet or Timimoun. We issue the authorisation (**€40**) and the visa sticker is issued on arrival; a separate sticker tax depends on your nationality.

Send us your nationality and travel dates and we will tell you which route applies. The details are on our [entry and e-Visa page](/evisa/) and in our [visa guide](/blog/algeria-visa-guide/).

## Time, power and money
- **Time:** Algeria runs on UTC+1 all year, with no daylight saving: **the same time as Denmark, Sweden and Norway in winter**, and one hour behind in summer. Finland is one hour ahead of Algeria in winter and two in summer.
- **Power:** Algeria uses the same **230 V power and Type C and F plugs** as the Nordic countries — no adapter needed.
- **Money:** Our prices are in euros, and you pay us before the trip. On the ground Algeria runs on cash: bring **euros** in clean notes to change — Nordic currencies cannot be changed in Algeria. Cards are rarely accepted outside big hotels — see [can I use credit cards in Algeria?](/questions/can-i-use-credit-cards-in-algeria/)

## When to go
The Sahara is travelled from **October to April** — exactly the dark months at home. Days in the desert are warm and bright, nights are cold and full of stars. **Christmas and New Year** there are special: see our [winter guide](/blog/christmas-new-year-in-algeria/).
