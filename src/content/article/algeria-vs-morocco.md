It's the most common question people ask when they start thinking about North Africa: **Algeria or Morocco?** Both are extraordinary, but they offer very different trips. Here's an honest comparison — from someone who runs trips in Algeria, so read the bias accordingly, but the facts below are straight.

## The short answer
- Go to **Morocco** if you want **easy, well-developed tourism** — souks, riads, a polished circuit you can do independently.
- Go to **Algeria** if you want a **wilder, far less-visited** trip: the best Roman cities in North Africa, a colossal empty Sahara, and almost no crowds.

## Tourism infrastructure and ease
Morocco has decades of tourism behind it: abundant riads, guides, and a well-trodden Marrakech–Fes–Sahara circuit. **Algeria is lighter** — fewer hotels, less English, and the deep south needs a licensed operator. Morocco is the easier choice for independent first-timers; Algeria rewards those who plan ahead.

## Roman heritage
No contest for ruins: **Algeria wins**. Timgad and Djémila are among the best-preserved Roman cities anywhere and you'll often have them nearly to yourself. Morocco's Volubilis is lovely but singular.

## The Sahara
Both have great desert, but they're different. Morocco's **Merzouga** dunes are accessible and set up for tourism. Algeria's **Tassili n'Ajjer** and **Tadrart** are more remote, more pristine, and richer in prehistoric rock art — a guided, winter expedition rather than a quick overnight.

## Crowds, cost and visas
- **Crowds:** Algeria has far fewer.
- **Cost:** both are cheap day-to-day; Algeria's big-ticket item is the guided Sahara, Morocco offers more budget-to-luxury range.
- **Visas:** Morocco is visa-free for many nationalities; **Algeria usually needs a visa arranged in advance** — see our [Algeria visa guide](/blog/algeria-visa-guide/).

## So which should you choose?
If it's your **first trip to North Africa** and you want ease and icons, Morocco is the safe pick. If you've "done" Morocco, or you want **depth, space and heritage without the crowds**, Algeria is the more rewarding trip — and the one we can help you plan.
