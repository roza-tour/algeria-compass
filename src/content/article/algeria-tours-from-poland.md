Algeria rewards the kind of traveller who is happy off the beaten track: it is one of the least-visited countries in the Mediterranean, and good value once you are there. Our [cost guide](/blog/algeria-travel-cost/) has the real numbers.

This page gathers what matters when you are travelling from Poland: getting there, the visa, time and money, when to go, and which of our private tours fit best. Every tour is private, on your own dates, with a licensed local guide. Our guides work in English (and French and German).

## Getting there
There is usually no direct flight from Poland, so plan on one connection into **Algiers (ALG)** through Paris, Frankfurt, Rome, Vienna or Istanbul. Total journey time from Warsaw or Kraków is typically 6 to 9 hours. Check current schedules when you book.

## The visa
Visitors from Poland need a visa for Algeria. There are two routes:

1. **A tourist visa from the Algerian embassy or consulate** that serves you, applied for before you travel. Consulates want an invitation letter and a confirmed programme from a licensed Algerian operator — we prepare both with any programme of **at least 3 days that includes airport pickup and drop-off** (the invitation letter is **$25**).
2. **The Saharan visa-authorisation**, for organised trips that include the Sahara, such as Djanet or Timimoun — whichever airport you fly into. We issue the authorisation (**$45**) and the visa sticker is issued on arrival at Algiers airport; the arrival stamp is a separate fee paid at the airport.

Send us your nationality and travel dates and we will tell you which route applies. The details are on our [entry and e-Visa page](/evisa/) and in our [visa guide](/blog/algeria-visa-guide/).

## Time, power and money
- **Time:** Algeria runs on UTC+1 all year, with no daylight saving: **the same time as Poland in winter**, and one hour behind in summer.
- **Power:** Algeria uses the same **230 V power and Type C and F plugs** as Poland — no adapter needed.
- **Money:** Our prices are in euros, and you pay us before the trip. On the ground Algeria runs on cash: bring **euros** in clean notes to change — złoty cannot be changed in Algeria. Cards are rarely accepted outside big hotels — see [can I use credit cards in Algeria?](/questions/can-i-use-credit-cards-in-algeria/)

## When to go
The Sahara is travelled from **October to April**. For travellers who like their trips with some effort in them, the [Sefar trek](/tours/sefar-tassili-trek/) — a week on foot across the Tassili plateau to the greatest prehistoric paintings in the Sahara — is the one to aim for.

The north of Algeria, the M'Zab Valley and the organised Sahara routes are calm and very welcoming; our [safety guide](/blog/algeria-safety-guide/) sets out the evidence.
