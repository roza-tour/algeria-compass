Most visitors picture Algeria as one thing: sand. It is the largest country in Africa, and its climates **stack** — Mediterranean coast, forested mountains, cold high plateaus, then the Sahara. In winter that stacking produces a genuinely unusual holiday: **you can stand in snow and, days later, in warm desert dunes.**

## Yes, it snows in Algeria

Every winter, the **Atlas ranges** hold real snow:

- **Chréa**, in the cedar forests above [Blida](/provinces/blida/) — roughly **an hour from Algiers**, with a small ski station.
- **Tikjda**, in the **Djurdjura** mountains of Kabylie — mountain walking, snow and another modest ski station.
- **The Aurès**, above [Batna](/provinces/batna/) — snowy peaks near the Roman city of [Timgad](/destinations/timgad/).

The **high plateaus** around [Sétif](/provinces/setif/) and Batna are also properly cold in winter, with occasional snow in the towns themselves.

These are **modest facilities**, not alpine resorts — but the snow, the cedars and the emptiness are real.

## The four zones, in one country

| Zone | What you get | Winter conditions |
|---|---|---|
| **Mediterranean coast** | Mild, green, walkable cities | ~10–17°C, some rain |
| **Mountains** | Cedar forest, snow, small ski stations | Near or below freezing |
| **High plateaus** | Roman cities, huge skies | Cold and crisp |
| **Sahara** | Dunes, rock art, desert camps | Warm days ~20–25°C, freezing nights |

## How to build the snow-to-Sahara week

The contrast only works **December to February**, when the mountain snow and the [prime Saharan season](/blog/algerian-sahara-travel-guide/) overlap.

1. **Algiers** (2 nights) — coast, the [Casbah](/destinations/casbah-of-algiers/), mild sea air.
2. **Chréa** (day trip or 1 night) — cedar forest and snow, about an hour inland.
3. **Fly south** to **Djanet** or **Tamanrasset** (3–4 nights) — guided 4x4, dunes, rock art, camps under an exceptional winter sky.
4. Optional: **the Roman east** (Timgad, Djémila) on cold, clear plateau days.

Fly the southern leg — the road distances are enormous. Our [transport guide](/blog/algeria-transportation-guide/) explains the logistics, and the [itinerary guide](/blog/algeria-itinerary/) has full-length plans.

## Packing for two climates at once

This is the part people get wrong. In the same bag you need:

- **Mountain-cold layers** — proper warm clothing, waterproof outer, decent shoes.
- **Desert kit** — strong sun protection by day, and warm layers again for **freezing desert nights**.

Full checklist in our [packing list](/blog/algeria-packing-list/).

## When it doesn't work

- **Spring** — the snow has largely gone; lovely for the north, no contrast.
- **Summer** — the deep desert is dangerously hot and effectively off-limits.

So this is a **winter-only trip** — which pairs neatly with [Christmas and New Year](/blog/christmas-new-year-in-algeria/), when the desert is at its best anyway.
