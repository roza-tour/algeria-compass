Algeria and Tunisia are neighbours with shared history — Roman, Arab, Ottoman, French — but they offer very different trips today. Here's an honest comparison. We run trips in Algeria, so weigh the bias accordingly; the facts below are straight.

## The short answer
- Go to **Tunisia** for an **easy, affordable** trip: visa-free entry for most nationalities, short flights from Europe, resort beaches and a well-trodden circuit.
- Go to **Algeria** for **scale and depth**: far fewer tourists, exceptional Roman cities, and a Sahara several times larger and wilder.

## Ease of entry and getting there
Tunisia is **visa-free for many nationalities** and heavily served by European budget and charter flights. **Algeria usually requires a visa arranged in advance** — see our [visa guide](/blog/algeria-visa-guide/) — and has fewer direct routes. If minimal friction matters most, Tunisia wins.

## Roman heritage
Both are exceptional. Tunisia has **Carthage** and the vast amphitheatre at **El Djem**. Algeria has **Timgad** and **Djémila**, which are among the best-preserved Roman cities anywhere — and you will often walk them almost alone. For preservation and solitude, Algeria edges it.

## The Sahara
This is the clearest difference. Tunisia's desert around **Douz** and **Tozeur** is accessible and easy to sample in a day or two. Algeria's Sahara is **another order of magnitude** — the **Tassili n'Ajjer**, the **Tadrart**, the **Hoggar** — remote, permit-based, guided, and unforgettable. See our [Sahara guide](/blog/algerian-sahara-travel-guide/).

## Beaches and resorts
Tunisia wins outright: **Djerba**, Hammamet and Sousse are built for beach holidays. Algeria's ~1,600 km of Mediterranean coast is beautiful but largely **undeveloped for tourism** — lovely to visit, not a resort destination.

## Crowds and cost
- **Crowds:** Algeria has dramatically fewer visitors.
- **Cost:** both are inexpensive day to day. Tunisia offers cheap packages; in Algeria the north is affordable and the guided Sahara is the big-ticket item — see our [cost guide](/blog/algeria-travel-cost/).

## So which should you choose?
Pick **Tunisia** for a straightforward, sun-and-ruins holiday, especially with family or on a first North African trip. Pick **Algeria** if you want **heritage without crowds** and a serious desert experience — and see how it compares with its western neighbour in [Algeria vs Morocco](/blog/algeria-vs-morocco/).
