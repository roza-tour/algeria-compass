What you pack for Algeria depends on where you're going — the northern cities and coast are one trip, the Sahara is another entirely. Here's a practical checklist that covers both, plus the things travellers most often forget.

## Clothing
Algeria is a majority-Muslim country, so **modest, breathable clothing** is the rule — cover shoulders and knees, especially outside the big cities.

- Lightweight layers (warm days, cool evenings)
- A **warm layer** — genuinely needed for desert nights and mountains
- A **headscarf** (useful for women, sun and sand)
- Comfortable **closed walking shoes**
- Modest swimwear for the coast

## Money and documents
- **Cash** — the single most important item. Bring **euros or dollars** to change.
- Your **visa paperwork** and passport, plus copies (paper and photo)
- Travel insurance details

## Health and daily kit
- Any **prescription medication** (plus copies of prescriptions)
- A small first-aid kit, sun protection, hand sanitiser
- A **refillable water bottle** (drink bottled water)
- A **power bank** and a type C/F, 230V adapter

## Sahara-specific
The desert catches people out because it's **cold at night** and intensely sunny by day:

- Warm layers and a windproof jacket
- High-SPF sunscreen, sunglasses, a brimmed hat
- A scarf/shemagh against sand
- A headtorch and spare batteries
