**Houari Boumediene Airport (ALG)** lies roughly twenty kilometres east of central Algiers. In light traffic the run into the city takes half an hour; in the Algiers rush it can take considerably longer, and the coast road into the centre is the bottleneck.

The **smoothest option is a pre-arranged transfer** — your hotel's car or your operator's driver, meeting you in arrivals with your name. Given that you will be landing with foreign cash and no dinars yet, and that [cards are barely usable](/questions/can-i-use-credit-cards-in-algeria/), having the transfer settled in advance removes the most awkward first hour of the trip.

**Official taxis** operate from the terminal. Algiers taxis do not reliably run on a meter for airport runs, so agree the fare before you get in rather than after you arrive; ask your hotel in advance what the going rate is so you have a number in mind. Note that drivers will want dinars.

There is also a **bus link** into the city, which is cheap and used by locals but awkward with luggage and not a comfortable introduction to a new country at the end of a long flight.

One practical sequencing tip: change a modest amount of money at the airport so you have dinars for the first day, then change the rest through your hotel or operator at a better rate — see [what currency does Algeria use?](/questions/what-currency-does-algeria-use/).
