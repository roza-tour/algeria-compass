Algeria's reputation was shaped by the 1990s, not by the country you will meet today. It has been calm and stable for over two decades, and what visitors remember is the hospitality: the coffee someone has already paid for, the stranger who walks you to the right bus, the invitation to share a family couscous.

**Easy:** Algiers, Oran, Constantine, the coast, Roman sites, Kabylie and the M'Zab.
**With a guide and permit:** the deep Sahara (Tassili, Tadrart, Hoggar), arranged for you.
**Not open to visitors:** a few remote areas along the far-south frontier.

Carry ID for routine checkpoints, dress modestly at religious sites, ask before photographing people, and let your guide plan the deep-south routes.
