Algeria has **seven UNESCO World Heritage Sites**:

1. **Al Qal'a of Beni Hammad** (inscribed 1980) — the ruined first capital of the Hammadid emirate, in the Hodna mountains.
2. **Tassili n'Ajjer** (1982) — a vast Saharan plateau famous for prehistoric rock art; listed for **both culture and nature**.
3. **M'Zab Valley** (1982) — five fortified Ibadi oasis towns around [Ghardaïa](/tours/ghardaia-guided-tour/).
4. **Djémila** (1982) — one of the best-preserved Roman towns in North Africa. See our [Djémila guide](/destinations/djemila/).
5. **Tipasa** (1982) — Roman and Punic ruins on the Mediterranean west of Algiers.
6. **Timgad** (1982) — a remarkably complete Roman colonial town near Batna.
7. **Kasbah of Algiers** (1992) — the historic Ottoman-era citadel and medina. See our [Casbah of Algiers guide](/destinations/casbah-of-algiers/).

Several of these feature directly in our [private tours](/tours/) — from the Roman north-east to the Saharan rock art of [Tassili n'Ajjer](/destinations/tassili-najjer/). For a fuller tour of all seven, see our [UNESCO sites of Algeria](/unesco/) guide.
