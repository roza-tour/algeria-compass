Algeria is a good-value destination. With **Algeria Compass**, a **private guided day tour starts from €30 per person**, and **multi-day private journeys** run from roughly **€200 to €1,200**, depending on how many days, which regions, the level of accommodation and the time of year. You can see exact, per-tour pricing on each [tour page](/tours/).

Beyond the tour itself, day-to-day costs in Algeria are modest compared with Europe: meals, local transport and everyday items are inexpensive. The currency is the **Algerian dinar (DZD)**. For a fuller breakdown of budgeting, see our [complete Algeria travel guide](/blog/complete-algeria-travel-guide/).
