For most travellers, **October to April** is the sweet spot: the northern cities, the Roman sites and the coast are pleasant, and the Sahara is comfortable rather than punishing.

- **Sahara (Djanet, Tassili, the M'Zab, Timimoun):** best **October to March**. Days are warm and clear, nights cool. Avoid **June–August**, when desert heat is extreme.
- **Coast and cities (Algiers, Oran, Béjaïa):** lovely in **spring and autumn**; summer is warm and good for the Mediterranean.
- **Mountains (Kabylie, the Aurès):** green in spring; some high areas see snow in winter.
