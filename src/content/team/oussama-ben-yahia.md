Oussama Ben Yahia is the founder and lead travel specialist behind Algeria Compass. He is based in Algeria and has built the company around a simple idea: that Algeria — its Sahara, its Roman and Islamic heritage, its mountains and coast — deserves to be understood and travelled well, by people who know the ground.

## On the ground in Algeria
Oussama designs and personally leads private journeys across the country, from the Tassili plateaus and the oases of the south to the UNESCO Roman cities of the north-east, the Kabylie coast, the M'Zab valley and the Andalusian west around Tlemcen. The guides he writes for this site come from that direct experience — the routes, the seasons, the practicalities and the judgement calls that only show up when you travel a place repeatedly.

## How he writes
Oussama writes the site's flagship travel guides and works with the Algeria Compass editorial process to keep them accurate: claims are checked against primary and official sources, dated, and revised on a schedule. He separates fact from practical judgement, and where something depends on conditions on the ground he says so rather than smoothing it over.

## What he covers
Trip planning and itineraries, Saharan travel and logistics, Algeria's seven UNESCO World Heritage sites, and the culture, history and food that give the country its character. His work is reviewed independently against the Algeria Compass [editorial standards](/editorial/).
