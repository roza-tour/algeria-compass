The Algeria Compass editorial team is responsible for every guide, province page, destination and answer published on this site. We are a small, Algeria-focused editorial operation rather than a content farm: we write about places we research carefully, in English, French and Arabic, for an international audience that wants to understand and travel Algeria well.

## What we cover
Our work spans the whole country — the Sahara and the Tassili plateaus, the UNESCO Roman cities of the north-east, the Kabylie coast and mountains, the Andalusian west around Tlemcen and Oran, the M'Zab valley, and the Mediterranean coast and capital. We focus on heritage, history, culture, food and the practical logistics of getting around.

## How we work
We research each topic against primary and authoritative sources, draft it with regional knowledge, verify factual claims, date the result and review it on a schedule. We separate **fact** (which we source) from **practical judgement** (our recommendations) and **opinion** (clearly framed as such). Where we are uncertain, we say so rather than guess.

## Accuracy over marketing
Algeria Compass is a travel business, but our editorial standard is independence from the sell: a guide's job is to be accurate and useful first. We do not invent reviews, credentials, ratings or partnerships, and we correct mistakes openly. Our full standards are set out in our [Editorial Policy](/editorial/editorial-policy/), [Research Methodology](/editorial/research-methodology/) and [Fact-Checking Policy](/editorial/fact-checking-policy/).

## Named authorship
As our team grows, individual writers and editors will be credited by name on the work they produce, with their own profiles, expertise and the content they have written and reviewed. This page represents the collective team that stands behind everything published here today.
